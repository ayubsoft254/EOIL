About the lab

Satellite data, in the hands of the community

The Earth Observation Innovation Laboratories (EOIL) project establishes innovation hubs at Taita Taveta University and the University of Nairobi. Commissioned by the European Union and France and implemented by Expertise France with the Kenya Space Agency, the Taita Taveta University lab gives students, researchers, county governments and entrepreneurs open access to geospatial data, processing tools and expert support.

From the plains of Tsavo to the Taita Hills, our region faces real challenges — drought, human–wildlife conflict, mining impacts and rapid urbanisation. Earth observation puts evidence behind every decision.

What you get at the lab

  • Free access to satellite imagery, geospatial datasets and processing workstations
  • Hands-on training in GIS, remote sensing and open-source EO tools
  • Prototyping sessions, hackathons and incubation for EO-based start-ups
  • Networking with the Kenya Space Agency, county stakeholders and industry
  • Technical support for research, county planning and civil-society projects

Our objectives

Five goals driving the Taita Taveta University hub

01

An operational innovation hub

A fully equipped physical and virtual lab at Taita Taveta University, open to students, researchers, counties and entrepreneurs.

02

Uptake & application of EO

Promoting the everyday use of satellite data in agriculture, planning, conservation and county decision-making.

03

A catalysed innovation ecosystem

Hackathons, incubation and prototyping that turn ideas into EO-powered products and start-ups.

04

Capacity building

Training programmes delivered through a Centre of Excellence model — from short courses to certified tracks.

05

Stakeholder technical support

Expert help for government, academia, civil society and the private sector applying EO to their mandates.

Focus areas

Where satellite data meets Taita Taveta

🌾

Agriculture & food security

Crop monitoring, drought early warning and precision-farming insights for smallholders in the county.

🌍

Climate resilience

Tracking rainfall, land degradation and water resources to help communities adapt to a changing climate.

🏙️

Urban & land-use planning

Evidence for county spatial plans — settlement growth, infrastructure and land-cover change in Voi and beyond.

⛏️

Mining & environment

Monitoring gemstone-mining impacts and land rehabilitation across Kenya's mining heartland.

🐘

Wildlife & conservation

Supporting Tsavo-ecosystem conservation with habitat mapping and human–wildlife-conflict analysis.

🛰️

Open EO tools & data

Building local skills in open-source geospatial software and free satellite-data catalogues.
